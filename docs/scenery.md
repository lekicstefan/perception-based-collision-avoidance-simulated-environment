# Scenery

The scenario scenes are low-poly towns: a road with sidewalks, buildings on both sides, trees, street lamps and street furniture, with vehicles and pedestrians as the moving objects. The models come from free asset packs (listed in `docs/assets.md`, not stored in this repository); everything else is made by two small editor scripts. The scenery is a picture for the camera and a set of simple shapes for the lidar. It is not tuned for either sensor.

## What is where

| Part | File | Runs |
|---|---|---|
| Wrapper prefabs, road and sky materials, showcase scene | `Assets/Editor/EnvironmentKit.cs` | in the editor only |
| Road ribbon (asphalt) | `Assets/Scripts/Routes/RouteRoadMesh.cs` | at scene start |
| Sidewalks, curbs, lane markings | `Assets/Scripts/Routes/RouteRoadDressing.cs` | at scene start |
| Buildings, trees, lamps, props along the road: settings | `Assets/Scripts/Routes/RoadsideScenery.cs` | nothing at run time |
| Buildings, trees, lamps, props along the road: generator | `Assets/Editor/RoadsideSceneryBuilder.cs` | in the editor only |
| Lists what the imported packs contain | `Assets/Editor/PackReport.cs` | in the editor only |

Generated by the kit (committed, contains no pack data): `Assets/Environment` (textures, materials, wrapper prefabs). The asset packs themselves live in `Assets/Asset Packs` and are excluded from git.

## The kit (`Tools > Environment Kit`)

| Menu item | What it does |
|---|---|
| Build kit | makes the road, sidewalk, curb, line and sky materials (asphalt speckle and sidewalk slab textures are drawn by code) and one wrapper prefab per catalogue entry in `Assets/Environment/Prefabs` |
| Build kit (reset orientation and scale) | the same, ignoring any rotation or scale that was changed by hand in the wrapper prefabs |
| Apply look to the open scene | sky, ambient light and one directional light |
| Add road dressing to selected road | adds `RouteRoadDressing` to a road object (`RouteRoadMesh`), assigns the kit materials, asphalt on the road |
| Create showcase scene | `Assets/Scenes/EnvironmentShowcase.unity`: every wrapper prefab in rows, to check size and facing |
| Generate roadside scenery for selected / Clear generated scenery of selected | see below |
| Report asset packs | writes `environment_pack_report.txt` to the project root: every prefab and model of the packs with size, colliders, shaders, plus textures and materials |

The catalogue (what is wrapped, with which label, collider and scale) is the list at the top of `EnvironmentKit.cs`. To add a model, add one line there.

## Wrapper prefabs

A wrapper is the pack model (a plain copy with its own colliders removed) as the child `Visual` of a root object. Name prefixes: `PD_` Pandazole City Town, `SP_` SimplePoly Town Pack, `KN_` Kenney Car Kit, `Pedestrian_` Banana Man.

* **Pivot on the ground**: the centre of the footprint for boxes; for people, trees, signs and poles the pivot stays where the model has it (its base).
* **One root collider** that matches the visible shape: a box fitted to the model, or an upright capsule for people, trees, signs and poles.
* **A `Hazard` component with a label, on the Hazard layer (7)**: the ego car collides with it, the ground truth log has it every physics step, the lidar sees it. The label never reaches the processor. The evaluation (8.8) uses the label to decide what counts as an object the system must avoid.
* Labels: `pedestrian_adult`, `pedestrian_child`, `car`, `van`, `truck`, `building`, `tree`, `bush`, `stone`, `traffic_cone`, `trash_bin`, `trash_bag`, `barrel`, `cabinet`, `sign`, `bench`, `dumpster`, `hydrant`, `mail_box`, `lamp_post`, `traffic_light`, `billboard`.

Details that were chosen on purpose:

| Item | Choice |
|---|---|
| Trees | the collider is a trunk-sized capsule (radius 0.2 m, 45 % of the height). The crown is a picture: the lidar passes through it and the log has no box for it. A flat green disc (1.8 m) lies around the trunk, without a collider |
| People | the Banana Man dummy: adult 1.75 m, child 1.1 m; arms swung down to an A pose; body tinted crash-test yellow; standing on a small wheeled base with a pole into the body, so it looks as if it rolls. No animation: it slides along its path |
| Other vehicles | Kenney Car Kit models, scaled by `KenneyScale` (1.7) so they match the ego car |
| Signs, lamps, traffic lights | thin capsule collider; the arm of a lamp sticks out of the collider |

### Fixing the orientation or the scale of a model

Models have no common forward axis. The kit assumes the front is +Z. To fix a model:

1. Double-click its prefab in `Assets/Environment/Prefabs` (prefab mode).
2. Select the child `Visual`, set Rotation Y (90, 180 or 270) and, if needed, Scale (all three equal). The front of a car is the side with the head lights, the blue arrow of the move tool is +Z.
3. Leave prefab mode and save, then run `Build kit`. The kit keeps the rotation and scale of `Visual` and fits the pivot and the collider again (a 90 degree turn swaps length and width, so the collider must be refitted).
4. All instances in all scenes follow. `Build kit (reset orientation and scale)` throws the changes away.

## The road

`RouteRoadMesh` builds the asphalt from the route file. **The route is the path of the ego car and never moves.** The road can be moved sideways against it with `Centre Offset M` (to the left of the route, negative = right). With `Width` 9 and `Centre Offset M` 2.25 (a quarter of the width) the route is the centre line of the right-hand lane, and the centre line of the road is 2.25 m to the left of the car. `RouteRoadDressing` and `RoadsideScenery` read both values from `RouteRoadMesh` on the same object, so there is one place to change them.

Texture coordinates of the ribbon are in metres (`Uv Tile M`, 4 m), so a texture does not stretch with the road's length.

`RouteRoadDressing` adds, on the same object:

* sidewalks on both sides (`Sidewalk Width`, curb 12 cm): one mesh on the Ground layer. The ego car never collides with it, the lidar sees it;
* a dashed centre line (3 m dash, 6 m gap) and edge lines, 2 cm above the road, with no collider (the lidar cannot see them, the camera can).

Junctions and crossing roads are separate road objects, each with its own route file; switch off a sidewalk where two roads overlap.

## Roadside scenery

`RoadsideScenery` (on the road object) holds the settings, the menu items `Generate roadside scenery for selected` and `Clear generated scenery of selected` make the objects. They become ordinary objects under a child called `Generated`, saved with the scene.

| Setting | Meaning |
|---|---|
| `Seed` | the same settings and seed always give the same scenery |
| `Start M`, `End M`, `Keep Clear M` | range along the route; `Keep Clear M` is a list of (from, to) ranges where nothing is placed, on either side |
| `Building Filter`, `Building Exclude` | which building prefabs are used: a name prefix (`PD_`, `SP_`, empty = all) and a list of names that are never used (default: the largest SimplePoly buildings 28 to 35) |
| `Building Setback M` | distance of the building fronts from the outer edge of the sidewalk. A negative value moves them onto the sidewalk edge, so that no gap is left |
| `Building Front Yaw` | extra turn of every building (only for models whose front was not fixed in the prefab) |
| `Tree Spacing M`, `Tree Jitter M`, `Tree Curb Offset M` | trees stand on the sidewalk near the curb |
| `Lamp Prefab`, `Lamp Spacing M`, `Lamp Offset M`, `Lamp Front Yaw` | street lamps alternate between the sides |
| `Props Per 100 M` | bins, hydrants, benches and mail boxes on the sidewalks |

The sidewalk width and the lane offset are taken from `RouteRoadDressing` and `RouteRoadMesh`. **Generating again replaces everything under `Generated`**, including objects that were moved by hand: generate once, then adjust. Fixes to the wrapper prefabs are kept.

Buildings are placed along the route, so on a sharp curve the buildings on the inside may overlap; use `Keep Clear M` or move them by hand.

## Rules the ground truth depends on

* One root collider per prefab; visual parts have no colliders.
* Every solid prefab is a `Hazard` on the Hazard layer; ground surfaces are on the Ground layer (3) and are not Hazards. The ego car only collides with the Hazard layer, the vehicle's ground ray uses only the Ground layer.
* Lane markings have no collider and are invisible to the lidar.

## Limits and open items

* **Log size**: `hazards.csv` has one row per Hazard per physics step. A generated street has dozens of static Hazards (buildings, trees, lamps). Static ones could be logged once per run (open item, not done).
* **Mixed styles**: Pandazole and SimplePoly differ in style. `Building Filter` limits a street to one pack.
* **Pedestrians slide**: no walking animation; this is accepted.
* **Pack files are not in the repository** (Asset Store licence). Scenes and wrapper prefabs refer to pack assets by their IDs, which stay the same when the same packs are imported again, so anyone with the packs can rebuild the scenes (see `docs/assets.md`).
* Results on this scenery say nothing about real-world camera or lidar behaviour.
